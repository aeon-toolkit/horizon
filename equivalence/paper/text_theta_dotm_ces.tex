aeon's \texttt{Theta}, \texttt{DOTM} and \texttt{AutoCES} are equivalent in
accuracy to the statsforecast implementations, and \texttt{AutoCES} also to
smooth's, on both simulated series and the full M4: every geometric mean MASE
ratio is within 1\% of one and equivalent by TOST with a 5\% margin
(Table~\ref{tab:other_stats}). They are also faster at every length tested:
at length 100 by factors of 1.9, 3.0 and 1.2 against statsforecast (31
against smooth), rising to 481, 69 and 3.0 (66) at length 10{,}000, where
statsforecast's \texttt{Theta} and \texttt{DOTM} take 1.9\,s and 5.6\,s per
series against 4\,ms and 81\,ms for aeon.
